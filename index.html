Three things to move ARIS from pilot to platform.

A named pilot team — to run ARIS against real, daily work.

Dedicated engineering time — to move it from prototype to production.

An executive sponsor — to champion adoption beyond the pilot.

Is the survey sample enough to act on?

It's a pulse check, not a statistically definitive study — but the top pain points and the top requested fixes line up closely with what ARIS already does, and that pattern has held as the sample has grown. The survey is still open.

What does ARIS do today versus the roadmap?

Today: it reads the project's pipelines and answers questions in plain language. Planned: automatic documentation, and eventually generating new pipelines from a written request — text-to-ARD build.

What would the pilot actually need from leadership?

A named pilot team, dedicated engineering time, and an executive sponsor to back adoption beyond that first team — the three items above.
